## WHAT IS HRD CORP

**Full name:** Human Resources Development Corporation
**Formerly:** HRDF — Human Resources Development Fund
**Rebranded:** 2021
**Governed by:** Pembangunan Sumber Manusia Berhad (PSMB) Act 2001
**Under:** Ministry of Human Resources Malaysia

Both terms refer to the same body. Use HRD Corp in all Nexor outreach — it is the current correct name.

---

## THE CORE MECHANISM

Companies pay a monthly levy. That money sits in a fund. They can claim it back to pay for approved employee training. Many companies pay the levy every month but never claim it — the money sits unused and is forfeited after 2 years.

```
1. EMPLOYER PAYS LEVY → monthly contribution to HRD Corp fund
2. EMPLOYEE GETS TRAINED → by an HRD Corp registered training provider
3. EMPLOYER CLAIMS BACK → submits claim through eTRiS portal, gets reimbursed
```

If the employer never claims — the money is forfeited after 2 years.

---

## WHO MUST REGISTER

| Company Size | Registration | Levy Rate |
|---|---|---|
| 10 or more Malaysian employees | Mandatory | 1% of monthly wages and fixed allowances |
| 5 to 9 Malaysian employees | Voluntary | 0.5% of monthly wages and fixed allowances |
| Below 5 employees | Not eligible | — |
| Government agencies, statutory bodies, NGOs | Exempt | — |

Penalty for non-registration: fine up to RM10,000 or imprisonment up to 1 year or both.

---

## HOW THE LEVY IS CALCULATED

```
Monthly levy = (Basic Salary + Fixed Allowances) x 1%
```

What counts: basic salary, fixed monthly allowances stated in contract.
What does NOT count: bonuses, commissions, variable allowances, overtime.
Payment deadline: 15th of the following month.
Who pays: the employer. Never deducted from employee salary.

### Real Example
Hotel with 200 staff, average salary RM2,500/month:
Monthly levy = RM2,500 x 200 x 1% = RM5,000/month
Annual levy = RM60,000/year sitting in their HRD Corp account, claimable for training.

## HOW CLAIMS WORK

### Step 1 — Use an HRD Corp Registered Provider
Training must be delivered by a provider registered with HRD Corp.
Nexor AI Institute must be a registered provider for the claim to be valid.
Verify Nexor AI Institute registration status before using this angle in any outreach.

### Step 2 — Apply for Grant BEFORE Training
Under SBL (Skim Bantuan Latihan) — the main scheme:
Employer applies through eTRiS portal before training happens.
HRD Corp approves the grant.
Training runs.
Employer submits claim with documents.

### Step 3 — Submit Claim
Documents required: attendance sheets, invoice, completion certificates.

### Step 4 — Get Reimbursed
HRD Corp reimburses from the employer's levy balance.

## THE FORFEITURE RULE — THE SALES HOOK

If an employer does not claim within 2 years — unused levy is forfeited. Gone.
Companies with levy balance over RM50,000 and utilisation below 50% face an additional 15% deduction under PLM policy.

This is why the angle works: the money is already there. They just need to claim it before they lose it.

## ACCURATE FRAMING IN OUTREACH

### What is accurate:
```
"We're an HRD Corp registered training provider, so your company can claim this back from your existing levy."
"Your company already pays into HRD Corp every month. This training comes out of that — no extra budget needed."
"If your levy is sitting unclaimed, you have two years before it is forfeited. This is a good use of it."
```

### What is NOT accurate:
```
"It's free" — not free, the company already paid the levy
"Your levy covers it automatically" — employer must apply for grant and submit claim first
"Just claim it back" — there is a process involving pre-approval and documentation
"HRDC certified" — correct term is "HRD Corp registered training provider"
```

### Correct terminology:
- Organisation: HRD Corp
- Old name still widely used: HRDF
- The contribution: HRD Corp levy or HRDF levy
- The claim process: training grant claim via eTRiS
- Provider status: HRD Corp registered training provider (not "certified")
