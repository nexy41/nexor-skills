## STRATEGIC CHOICES

### Department Match
**HR — HR Data Flow Map (Capture → Record → Transfer → Externalize → Report)**

From workflowskill cross-reference:
- Capture stage: approvals via WhatsApp/email break structured flow
- Record stage: Excel as fallback control layer
- Report stage: management views require consolidation

Pain point: Approval evidence lives in fragmented channels instead of one controlled place.

### Hook Formula
**Formula 3 — Industry Observation**

Pattern from Example 5 (Mohammad Ali Mohd Hassan):
- Present tense first-person internal voice: "Most CPOs I speak to say..."
- Drop reader into moment they recognise
- Universally relatable, never punch down

### Outcome Formula
**Formula C — Direct Outcome**

"[Specific AI action] so [concrete result for this person]."

Pattern: Names solution without explaining what chatbot does (credentialed prospect knows). Shows concrete outcome they can picture.

### Delivery Mode
**In-house + Scope Broadening**

Reasoning:
- 500+ staff = enterprise scale
- Cannot leave floor = cannot do online training
- GLC context = in-house preferred for compliance/security
- "Any departments to use these tools" opens multi-department conversation

### Greeting
**Hello [Name]**

CPO = Director level and above per greeting table. Slightly more formal but warm.

### CTA
**Malaysian Spoken Variation**

"If this is worth the try just leave me a message."

From pattern learnings:
- Casual but professional
- Natural for Malaysian business register
- Less stiff than "Reply yes if this is useful"

### Language Register
**Malaysian spoken English**

Pattern learnings applied:
- "one place" not "one controlled place" — simpler word
- "what and when" ending — conversational rhythm
- Natural phrasing over grammatically perfect
