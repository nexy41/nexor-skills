# Nexor AI — Sales Directory

This directory contains the operational skills, templates, and guidelines for the Nexor AI sales team.

## Directory Structure

### `/cold-outreach`
The complete system for generating hyper-personalized, non-slop cold emails and multi-touch follow-up sequences.
- `nexor_outreach_guide.md` — The master operational pipeline for generating outreach.
- `cold_outreach_skill.md` — The core logic, formulas, and 8-step process for initial emails and follow-ups.
- `workflowskill.md` — The trigger event matrix and pain point mapping.
- `humanizer_skill.md` — The strict anti-slop engine that removes AI artifacts.
- `crm_skill.md` — The data formatter for logging outreach angles and follow-up dates in the CRM.
- `hrdcorp_skill.md` — Handling the HRD Corp "friction remover" messaging.
- `/examples/` — Successfully generated templates.

### `/befriending-strategy`
The social selling pipeline. Used for engaging prospects over a 1-week timeline as a peer/coach rather than a vendor, culminating in a soft close.
- `befriending_guide.md` — The operational 1-week timeline (Day 1, 3, 5, 7).
- `befriending_skill.md` — The DM generation prompt enforcing strict anti-slop, casual messaging rules.

## Core Philosophy: The Slop Spectrum
An email fails if it's too polished (AI slop) OR too cold (transactional). 
**The sweet spot is bluntness combined with exactly ONE human touch** (a hedge, a real reference, or a warm CTA). Never more than one.
