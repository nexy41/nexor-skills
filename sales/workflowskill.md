## THE 7 DEPARTMENTS

---

### 1. HR
**6 boards · 52 steps**

**Workflows:** Hiring · Onboarding · Payroll · Employee records · Industrial relations · HR reporting

**Current state — what they do today:**
- Leave, attendance, and training tracked in separate Excel files
- Approvals sent via personal WhatsApp
- HR chases every department before every payroll cut-off
- Onboarding depends on whichever manager is available
- IR records in physical files with no trail

**AI handles:**
- Approval routing without manual chasing
- Payroll data validation before cut-off
- Contract renewal and probation date alerts
- Onboarding checklists triggered on day one
- Policy chatbot — new hires ask about leave, claims, and working hours; the chatbot answers from the company's own policy documents; HR stops answering the same 10 questions every time someone joins

**Humans keep:**
Hiring decisions · Disciplinary actions · IR case handling · Performance judgments

**First wins (low risk, high impact):**
Leave approval routing · Payroll cut-off reminders · Policy chatbot for onboarding

**Best outreach angle:**
Approval delays, payroll errors, onboarding inconsistency — felt daily, no education required.
Policy chatbot — "Every time a new hire joins, HR answers the same 10 questions. A chatbot built on your own policy documents handles all of that."

### 2. Finance
**6 boards · 59 steps**

**Workflows:** Accounts payable · Accounts receivable · Staff claims · Month-end close · Tax compliance · Budget tracking

**Current state:**
- Finance matches PO → GRN → invoice manually in Excel every cycle
- Payment runs require multiple bank portal logins
- 3–5 working days lost every month-end chasing documents
- SST filing approached with incomplete supporting documents

**AI handles:**
- Invoice 3-way matching (PO → GRN → invoice)
- Bank reconciliation checks
- Statutory deadline alerts
- Claims verification against policy

**Humans keep:**
All payment approvals · Tax judgments · Budget reallocation · Audit sign-off

**Best outreach angle:**
Month-end close pressure every single month. Invoice matching errors and tax evidence gaps.

## TRIGGER EVENT MATRIX (Precision Targeting)

Do not just use titles; search for "hidden problems" indicated by public trigger events before sending outreach. If a trigger event exists, tie the pain point directly to it.

| Trigger Event | Where to Find It | What It Means (Hidden Problem) | Outreach Angle |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Recent Funding/Expansion** | News, LinkedIn | Rapid hiring = broken onboarding and approval bottlenecks. | "As you double headcount this year, manual onboarding in HR will break." |
| **New C-Level Hire (e.g., new CPO/CFO)** | LinkedIn | Mandate to fix controls and compliance within first 90 days. | "New leaders usually inherit undocumented approval chains in WhatsApp." |
| **New Software Implementation** | Job Postings | Integration headaches and adoption resistance. | "Most teams don't want to learn the new system; AI bridges the gap." |
| **Recent M&A** | News | Merging two different sets of manual processes. | "Aligning two different manual workflows takes months. Let AI standardize it." |
